# Three-Layer Persona Taxonomy

*Authored for this plugin. The canonical persona model used across portfolio classification, gap mining, and synthesis.*

## The Principle
A persona is not one thing. It is **three concentric layers**: who you're speaking to (Macro), what's actually driving them (Micro), and how the message reaches them as a distinct Andromeda signal (Vehicle). Two prospects under the same Macro can need completely different creative — because their Micro layer (desire, fear, belief) is different. Two micros can need the same vehicle, or the same micro can be hit by multiple vehicles to unlock incremental reach.

## In Plain English
Most persona work stops at "Layer 1": a demographic bucket like "Women 30+" or "Male Protein Gym-Goer". That's enough to pick a media plan, not enough to brief a creative. The real strategic work happens at **Layer 2** — naming the dominant *desire*, *fear*, and *belief* under the macro — because that's what determines which words land. **Layer 3** then says: same insight, multiple vehicles = multiple net-new audiences, because Meta's Andromeda treats different formats / creator types / shot grammars as different ads even when the message is the same.

> Two people under the same Macro persona can have completely different micro-drivers. Same product. Completely different brief.

## The Three Layers

### Layer 1 — Macro Persona (the WHO)
The broad type of person or state you're speaking to. Encompasses multiple Micro-personas beneath it.

- Format: a named archetype with life-stage / role / behavior markers.
- Example: *"The Male Protein Gym-Goer"*, *"The Perimenopausal Mom Returning to Running"*, *"The Late-20s Remote Worker Stuck on Slack"*.
- Failure mode: pure demographic ("Women 30+", "Millennials", "Health-Conscious Consumers"). A Macro must be specific enough that you can picture one in your head.

### Layer 2 — Micro Persona (the WHY)
The dominant motivator, core tension, and belief system that operates beneath the demographic surface. Where creative strategy actually lives.

Each Micro must be populated at three sub-fields:

- **Desire** — what this person actively wants. Not a benefit of the product; the underlying psychological pull.
- **Fear** — what they're trying to avoid. Often loss-framed (per `fear-of-loss-vs-desire-for-gain.md`).
- **Belief** — the implicit assumption that shapes how they interpret category messaging.

Example — two Micros under *The Male Protein Gym-Goer*:

| | **The Taste Realist** | **The Identity Keeper** |
|---|---|---|
| **Desire** | Enjoyment without compromise | Be seen as serious about fitness |
| **Fear** | Protein always tastes like crap | Losing the athletic identity built in their 20s |
| **Belief** | Taste and performance can't coexist | Supplements are for serious athletes |

Same product. Completely different creative brief, hook visual, vehicle.

A Micro should also carry:
- **Implied Reiss desire** (single best fit from `reiss-16-desires.md`)
- **Awareness stage** (Schwartz, from `schwartz-5-stages.md`) — what they currently believe / don't believe

### Layer 3 — Creative Vehicle (the HOW)
The format, creator type, tone, and visual execution that delivers the Micro's message as a distinct signal Andromeda can match against incremental audience pockets.

Vehicle dimensions overlap with the 9 Creative Diversity dimensions but resolve to a higher-level package:

- **UGC creator** — relatable demo, face-to-camera, authentic delivery
- **Podcast format** — founder interview, objection handling, long-form trust building
- **Static / motion** — bold visual + overlay text + product-led
- **Founder VSL** — direct-to-camera category educator
- **Reddit-style static** — text-as-image, problem-articulation
- **Listicle** — "5 things I wish I'd known"
- **Before-after** — proof-led, transformation framing

Same Micro can ship in multiple Vehicles. Each Vehicle earns a new Entity ID per the rule of pairs and unlocks a distinct audience pocket Andromeda can route to.

## How to apply

When **classifying portfolio ads** (Phase 1):
- Tag each ad with its implied Macro, Micro (desire / fear / belief), and Vehicle.
- Cluster ads first by Micro (not Macro) — that's where coverage actually concentrates or thins out.
- Empty cells in the Macro × Micro × Vehicle cube are your within-portfolio gaps.

When **mining for gaps** (Phase 2, any source — Reddit, X, reviews, surveys):
- A candidate gap must be specified at all three layers: Macro + Micro (desire/fear/belief) + recommended Vehicle(s).
- Verbatim quotes must articulate the Micro's *belief* or *fear* (per `pain-is-the-pitch.md`), not just topic affinity.
- Multiple Micros can live under one new Macro — surface them as siblings, not duplicates.

When **synthesizing** the final brief:
- Render the coverage matrix as Macro → Micros → Vehicles. Flat persona × format tables hide the strategic layer.
- Each Top Gap renders the same way, with the concept brief pulling explicitly on the Micro's belief/fear/desire triplet for hook framing.

## What does NOT count

- A persona named only by demographic ("Women 35–54") — that's a media planning bucket, not a Macro.
- A Micro that lists only a desire OR a fear, not all three of desire/fear/belief.
- A Micro that paraphrases the product's benefit as the "desire" (e.g. "wants smoother skin" — that's the product, not the underlying pull).
- A Vehicle field that says only "video" or "static" — that's format, not vehicle. The vehicle includes creator type and shot grammar.
- Two "different" personas that share Macro, Micro AND Vehicle — that's one persona with cosmetic variation, not coverage diversity.

## Anti-patterns

- **Stopping at Macro.** Treating the demographic as the brief. Produces generic creative that wins no specific Micro.
- **Single-vehicle thinking.** Locking a Micro to one Vehicle and missing the Andromeda reach unlock. The Identity Keeper Micro can ship as UGC, podcast, and static — three Entity IDs, three audience pockets.
- **Cosmetic Micro splits.** "She wants relief from bloating" and "She wants to feel comfortable in jeans" — same Micro, different surface phrasings. Real Micro splits diverge on belief.
- **Conflating Vehicle with Format.** Format is UGC / static / video. Vehicle is the *type* of UGC (founder interview vs friend-to-camera vs creator-with-product).
- **Skipping the belief.** Desire + fear without the underlying belief is half a Micro. The belief is what determines whether your hook lands or bounces off.
