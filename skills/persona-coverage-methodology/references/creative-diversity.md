# Creative Diversity

*Source: extracted from `the-edge-wiki/Frameworks/Creative Diversity.md`.*

## The Principle
Under Meta's Andromeda delivery model, **creative similarity is a penalty and creative diversity is a reward** — the visual differentiation across your ad library is now the single biggest performance lever you control.

## In Plain English
Meta no longer relies on audience targeting to decide who sees what — it relies on behavioral signals matched against the visual variety in your creative library (Soulmate Theory). If your ads look similar, the algorithm treats them as the same ad (Entity ID grouping), sees no reason to expand reach, and quietly caps performance. If your ads vary by format, persona, setting, and hook visual, the algorithm has more "candidates" to match to more audience pockets — and CPMs drop, reach broadens, learning accelerates.

> "If your ads all look similar, you're effectively showing the algorithm the same person over and over again. It has no reason to expand reach, because it sees no difference between one ad and the next."

## What Counts As Diversity (the 9 dimensions)

### Visual diversity (6 dimensions)
1. **Format** — UGC, static, hybrid, video, carousel, collection
2. **Persona** — who's on camera, who's the implied speaker, demographic
3. **Setting / backdrop** — bathroom vs. street vs. studio vs. kitchen
4. **Shot type** — flat-lay vs. handheld vs. talking-head vs. POV
5. **Hook visual** — what fills the first 3 seconds (see First 3 Seconds Rule)
6. **Vehicle** — testimonial, founder VSL, podcast clip, Reddit-style static, listicle

### Messaging diversity (3 dimensions)
7. **Emotional zone** — high positive, calm positive, subtle negative, urgent negative
8. **Identity angle** — actual self, ideal self, ought self
9. **Creative angle** — education, objection handling, myth busting, trust building

## What Does NOT Count As Diversity
- Headline edits
- Copy rewrites
- Color or layout tweaks
- New CTA text
- A new caption with the same visual

These don't earn a new Entity ID and therefore generate no new learning.

## How To Apply
1. Apply the **Persona x Format Grid** before a sprint: at minimum 3 personas × 3 formats with 3 hook visuals and 2 thumbnails per cell
2. Adopt **bi-weekly iteration sprints** instead of weekly micro-edits
3. Allocate ~50% of creative effort to **net-new concepts** and ~50% to **big-swing iterations** of winners (new vehicle, new persona, new hook)
4. Track **new Entity ID volume**, **unique reach**, **cold CPMs**, and **fresh-persona connection rate** — not just CTR/CPA
5. Pre-launch gut check: *"Would Meta think this looks new?"*

## Empirical evidence
**25 ad variants outperformed 5 ad variants** by 171 purchases despite reaching 59,000 fewer people, at **15% lower CPA** (Soar With Us internal incrementality test). Partnership-ads + third-party identity creative drove **800 incremental conversions** vs **520 BAU** at $25.63 vs $39.34 cost per incremental conversion.

## Anti-Patterns
- Trying to "fake diversity" with headline/copy/CTA permutations on the same visual
- Producing all assets in the same shoot location and lighting
- Optimising for Creative ID count instead of Entity ID yield
