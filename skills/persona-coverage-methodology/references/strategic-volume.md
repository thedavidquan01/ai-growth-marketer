# Strategic Volume

*Source: extracted from `the-edge-wiki/Frameworks/Strategic Volume.md`.*

## The Principle
Creative volume must equal *diversity*, not repetition — the unit that scales is volume of difference, because identical assets collapse into one Entity ID and earn no fresh learning, audience, or reach.

## In Plain English
Producing 50 near-identical assets is not high volume in the eyes of an algorithmic creative system — they get treated as one ad. The win condition is a high quantity of *visually distinct* ads, so the system has enough range to find the right soulmate creative for every audience pocket. More inputs only matter if they are different inputs.

## Key Facts
- Andromeda's capacity jumped from ~50 ads/week to ~5,000 ads/week, raising the ceiling on how many distinct assets can be productively absorbed
- Flooding that capacity with sameness wastes the headroom — every new asset must be visually distinct enough to earn its own Entity ID
- The four variables that govern monthly distinct-asset production volume are Win Rate, Half-Life, Churn Rate, and Scenario Planning — together the Volume & Velocity math
- 25 ad variants outperformed 5 variants by 171 purchases despite reaching 59,000 fewer people, at 15% lower CPA
- Partnership-ads + third-party identity creative drove 800 incremental conversions vs 520 BAU
- Cost per incremental conversion: $25.63 with partnership-ads vs $39.34 BAU

## How To Apply
1. Track **new Entity ID volume** as a primary KPI, not Creative ID count
2. Brief for visual range up front: format × persona × setting × hook visual × vehicle
3. Build a Persona x Format Grid — e.g. 3 personas × 3 formats × multiple hook visuals
4. Use the Testing Hierarchy Pyramid to bias production toward big swings, not micro-tweaks
5. Capture both video and native statics in the same shoot to compound output per production day
6. Use AI for background swaps and product renders — variation without reshoots
7. Run the Squint Test before launch to verify visual distinctiveness
8. Whitelist via partnership ads under creator handles — often gets unique Entity ID treatment
9. Calculate monthly distinct-asset target using Volume & Velocity math

## Anti-Patterns
- **Vanity volume**: shipping 100+ Creative IDs that collapse into 8 Entity IDs
- **Micro-tests as the primary motion**: swapping headlines, colors, layouts — these don't earn new Entity IDs
- **"Another kitchen scene"**: identical settings/personas across multiple shoots — the diversity gap is created at the brief stage, not the edit stage
- **Low volume, high diversity**: insufficient learning velocity; the system has too few candidates to find soulmates
- **High volume, low diversity**: everything collapses into a few Entity IDs; capped reach despite spend
