LinkedIn Post (Verified Version)

I Gemini-verified every ad in a brand's Meta portfolio. What metadata told me was completely wrong.
We built a system that audits a brand's full Meta ad portfolio for creative coverage gaps. Tested it on Grüns (greens gummy supplement). 360 ads across adult + kids pages. Step 1 was metadata analysis: pull every ad, classify by headline, landing page, format tag. The metadata said "DCO carousel" on 80% of ads. We classified them as brand-produced product carousels. Confidently wrote "zero UGC creator testimonials across 438 ads." Then we ran every ad through Gemini video analysis. The metadata was catastrophically wrong. Those "DCO carousels" contain UGC creator videos inside the carousel cards. ~70% of the portfolio is real women talking to camera in their kitchens. 18 distinct men on camera. Named doctors. Celebrity partnerships. The portfolio is UGC-heavy, not brand-produced. The lesson for anyone building creative analysis tools: Format tags lie. "DCO" doesn't mean "brand-produced." "VIDEO" doesn't mean "talking-head." "IMAGE" doesn't mean "no person." The only way to know what's in an ad is to watch it. What DID survive full verification: - 0 adult Founder VSL across 360 ads. Kids has founder content. Adult does not. Competitors AG1, Seed, Pendulum all run this format. - DCO cards are near-duplicates ~90% of the time. Same video in both carousel slots. 360 ads = ~80-100 distinct concepts after deduplication. - Heavy asset reuse. Same video running under 2-3 different ad IDs confirmed repeatedly. - OLIPOP Kids collab = 34% of the kids portfolio, functionally 3-4 visual concepts across 75+ ads. The gap isn't missing personas or missing UGC. It's one specific vehicle (Founder VSL) absent from the adult portfolio while every competitor runs it. Metadata analysis tells you what the ad manager set up. Gemini tells you what the viewer actually sees. Those are different things.
Methodology: 371 ads pulled from Meta Ad Library (Grüns page 107585658730958 + Grüns Kids page 738568902671176), sorted by total impressions. 360 verified via meta-get-ad + Gemini analyze-creatives across 37 parallel agent batches, 7 waves. ~450 carousel cards directly analyzed by Gemini; ~270 inferred from sibling card context + ad metadata. Gemini quota limits prevented full video analysis on some later batches; those cards were classified from thumbnails, transcripts, and paired-card inference. All claims marked "TRUE" in the scorecard are supported by direct Gemini observation, not metadata inference.
