Gap Priority — Ranked by Conversion Probability

Within-portfolio vehicle fills on proven audiences first · Net-new persona tests second

Scoring Model

Factor 1: Audience Proven
50%

Is the brand already spending on this Macro and converting? Within-portfolio fills (existing audience, new vehicle) always outrank net-new Macros (unproven audience).

Factor 2: Purchase Urgency
30%

How close is this Micro to buying? Acute problem states (GLP-1 side effects, constipation crisis) > ambient wellness. Already on medication > considering change.

Factor 3: Vehicle Category Proof
20%

Does this vehicle type convert in DTC supplements? UGC testimonial and Founder VSL are category-proven at AG1/Seed/Pendulum. Reddit static is low-cost/high-test-velocity.
